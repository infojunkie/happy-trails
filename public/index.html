Search syntax

A plain list of keywords searches every field as one contiguous phrase. Add a field prefix to narrow the search, quote field values that contain spaces or parentheses, and use AND or OR to combine terms independently.

album:
Album title and raw source titles.
artist:
Album artist, parsed artists, and track artists.
composer:
Composer tags from the tracks. Alias: composers:.
source:
Raw source/disc titles only. Alias: disc:.
trackartist:
Track-level artists only. Aliases: guest:, track_artist:.
all:
Every indexed field. Aliases: any:.

Operators

  • AND requires both sides; adjacent terms in a fielded expression are implicitly ANDed.
  • OR matches either side.
  • abbey road is a phrase; use abbey AND road for independent words.
  • AND binds more tightly than OR; parentheses group expressions.
  • Matching is case-, accent-, and ligature-insensitive, including quoted phrases.

Examples

Unknown field names are treated as ordinary text. Incomplete or malformed expressions fall back to the all-field token search. NOT is not supported.
